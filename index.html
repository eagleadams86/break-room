Break Room

Break Room

A room your team drops into beside the call you already have open: see who's around, then play a round of trivia together. No account and nothing to install — pick a nickname, then start a room or join one with its six-letter code.

Your nickname stays in this browser. A room, and everything said in it, is gone within a day of the night it was opened.

This copy of Break Room isn't connected to its cloud yet, so a room can't be started or joined from here. The steps are in the README.

Try a Demo Room opens a room of invented players who answer on their own, so you can see the lobby and play a round before inviting anyone real. Nothing in it is saved.

🔒 Your nickname stays in this browser. A room lives in the cloud for the evening — anyone holding its code can see it — and is deleted within a day.

Back Up & Restore

One JSON file holding your nickname and any custom question packs you have written — a copy you keep, independent of this browser. Rooms are never in it: a room lives in the cloud for an evening and is not yours to keep.

Restoring replaces the packs and nickname in this browser. You are told what the file holds and asked to confirm first.

Forget This Browser

Deletes your nickname, your custom packs and the list of recent rooms from this browser, and leaves any room you are in. Your theme is kept.

Invite Someone

Read the code out over the call, or send the link — it opens Break Room on this room. Anyone who has it can join, so share it with the people you want in the room.

Custom Packs

Your own questions, kept in this browser (and in a backup). Paste one question per line as question | a | b | c | d | correct, where correct is A–D or 1–4. Lines that can’t be read are named rather than dropped.
